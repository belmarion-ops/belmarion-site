The membership editions

Your way into Belmarion.

Distinct approaches to one personal philosophy. Every edition includes access to Vectors, Discovery, Reality Map and nomination privileges.

01 / FLEXIBLE INTRODUCTION

The Discovery Edition

A considered introduction over 90 days, with the freedom to continue through renewed terms by mutual agreement. Personal calibration, curated opportunities and room to explore without an annual commitment.

02 / ANNUAL PERSONAL MEMBERSHIP

The Signature Edition

An extended personal relationship, evolving throughout the year. With refreshed opportunities, responsive recalibration and continued access to the wider world of Belmarion.

03 / SHARED PRIVATE MEMBERSHIP

The Family Edition

Created for family offices and closely connected principals. Individually calibrated access within a more generous, flexible shared allocation, supported through a single private relationship.

04 / BESPOKE ORGANISATIONAL ACCESS

The Enterprise Edition

For organisations wishing to extend Belmarion to selected executives. A tailored arrangement shaped around the people involved, their requirements and the level of access envisioned.

Membership terms, suitability and arrangements are discussed privately. An expression of interest does not constitute admission.
