Privacy

Belmarion Operations Ltd respects the privacy of principals, prospective principals, representatives and other individuals with whom it interacts. This notice explains how Belmarion handles personal information.

WHO WE ARE

Belmarion Operations Ltd is the controller responsible for the personal information described in this notice. We are registered in Scotland under company number SC904426. Our registered office is Office 708, 18 Young Street, Unit LGE, Edinburgh, EH2 4JB, United Kingdom. Privacy enquiries and requests may be sent to private@belmarion.com.

INFORMATION WE MAY PROCESS

Depending on your relationship with Belmarion, we may process identity and contact information, including details submitted through our website enquiry form; membership, access and account records; device and security information; communications with us; information supplied while using a Belmarion service; service preferences and interaction records; and limited technical records needed to protect, operate and troubleshoot our systems, including measures against misuse of our enquiry form. We may also receive information from a representative, professional adviser, referring party or publicly available source where appropriate.

WHY WE PROCESS IT

We process personal information to respond to enquiries, administer private admissions and memberships; establish and protect identity, access and devices; provide and improve Belmarion services; communicate with principals and authorised representatives; maintain security, prevent misuse and investigate incidents; keep appropriate business and compliance records; and meet legal obligations.

LEGAL BASES

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, our processing may be necessary to take steps at your request or perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, or pursue legitimate interests such as operating and securing Belmarion, administering private admissions, improving our services and protecting our rights and those of our principals. Where a different legal basis is required for particular processing, including consent where applicable, we will identify it at the relevant point.

DISCLOSURE

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information only where appropriate to service providers acting for us, professional advisers, authorities where legally required, or other parties where necessary to provide a service you request or where you direct or authorise the disclosure. Access is limited according to purpose and role.

INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Belmarion operates internationally. Personal information may therefore be processed in countries other than the country in which it was collected. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, Belmarion uses an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism or other recognised safeguard.

RETENTION

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including service delivery, security, dispute resolution, record-keeping and legal or regulatory requirements. Retention periods vary according to the nature and sensitivity of the information, the relevant service and applicable obligations.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of or portability of personal information; to object to certain processing; to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and to complain to an applicable data-protection authority. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

UK, EEA AND SWITZERLAND

Individuals in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland may exercise the rights available under the data-protection law applicable to them. UK individuals may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. EEA and Swiss individuals may contact the competent supervisory authority in their jurisdiction.

UNITED STATES

Residents of US states with applicable consumer privacy laws may have additional rights concerning their personal information, subject to the scope, thresholds and exemptions of the relevant law. Where such a law applies to Belmarion, we will honour the rights it provides and will not discriminate against an individual for exercising them. Belmarion does not use this public website for behavioural advertising and does not sell personal information.

CANADA

Where Canadian private-sector privacy law applies, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) or applicable provincial legislation, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, an explanation of its source, use and disclosure, and correction of information that is inaccurate or incomplete. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice.

Send requests or privacy concerns to private@belmarion.com. If your concern is not resolved, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the relevant provincial privacy authority, as applicable.

AUSTRALIA

Where the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles apply to Belmarion, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you and correction of information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

Send requests or privacy complaints to private@belmarion.com. We will consider your complaint and respond within a reasonable period. If you are dissatisfied with our response, or have not received one within a reasonable period, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the matter falls within its jurisdiction.

THIS WEBSITE

This public corporate website is intentionally limited. It does not use advertising technology or behavioural analytics. If you submit an enquiry using the contact form, the information you provide is delivered to our Private Office using a contracted email service. We use limited technical checks to protect the form against abuse. Please do not submit admission credentials, payment details or sensitive calibration information through the public form. If Belmarion later introduces technologies or processing that require additional notice or consent, the relevant information and controls will be provided before that processing occurs.

CHANGES

We may update this notice as Belmarion's services and legal obligations develop. Material changes affecting existing processing will be communicated where required. This notice is effective 1 October 2026. Last updated 19 October 2026.
