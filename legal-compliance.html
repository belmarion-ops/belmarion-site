NATURE OF THE SERVICE

Belmarion operates a private membership focused on individual calibration, discovery and carefully researched recommendations, known as Vectors.

A Vector is a proposal for the principal's independent consideration. Belmarion does not act as a booking agency, concierge, experience operator or financier, and does not undertake third-party fulfilment.

INDEPENDENT ARRANGEMENTS

Any subsequent investigation, permission, booking, payment, travel planning, safety assessment or fulfilment is arranged independently by the principal or an authorised representative. Third-party access, availability and outcomes are not guaranteed by Belmarion.
